How it works

From request to confirmed appointment

  1. You send the request

    Name, how to reach you, what needs doing and any dates that suit. That's it.

  2. I come back with a quote

    Scope and price in writing, plus a question or two if anything is unclear. I aim to do this the same business day.

  3. You confirm the time

    Choose a window that works. The appointment is only booked once you've confirmed it back.

  4. The work gets done

    I turn up in the agreed window, do what the quote says, and confirm the finished work with you.
